### 3.1 Which strategy goes in circles

The headline count means nothing on its own — it could reflect the detector working, or merely that one strategy happened to produce the only trajectories deep enough to try. `eligible` is the column that settles it.

| Strategy | Trajectories | Eligible (≥3) | LOST fires | Rate of eligible | Distinct tasks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `l1_blind_retry` | 182 | 93 | **10** | 10.8% | 4 |
| `l2_5_diagnose_revise` | 32 | 0 | **0** | — | 0 |
| `l2_reflection` | 178 | 92 | **23** | 25.0% | 11 |

**Exposure is matched and the result is not.** Blind retry had 93 eligible trajectories and LOST fired on 10; reflection had 92 and it fired on 23.

This matters more than the count. §5's reflection result is an exact McNemar test on *apply outcomes*. This is a **different measure on different data** — Jaccard novelty over patch content, which never looks at whether a patch applied — and it points the same way: reflection makes the model re-propose its own previous edit. Two independent measures agreeing is considerably stronger than either alone, and the mechanism (`LIMITATIONS.md` §8 item 2: the model quotes its own previous attempt) predicts exactly this signature.

**The same clustering caveat applies, and it must be stated with the result.** The 23 firings come from only **11 distinct tasks** across repeats, so they are not 23 independent observations — the same weakness `LIMITATIONS.md` §3 records for the McNemar result. Treat this as corroboration of a narrow finding, not as a broad one.

## 4. What this does not establish

1. **No row here is test-verified.** Every count is over `verification: "apply"`, so the unit is *did the patch reach the repository*, not *did it fix the bug* (`LIMITATIONS.md` §1).
2. **Novelty is computed from patches only.** Attempts without a patch are excluded, not scored as zero-novelty, and the count is published above. Substituting the raw response would redefine the measure mid-study.
3. **These rows and the archive's are not interchangeable.** The archive can see LOGIC and cannot see LOST; this sweep is the reverse. Neither set is the superset.
